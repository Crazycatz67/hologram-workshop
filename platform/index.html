Controls

Library (L): ←/→ (or drag, scroll, swipe) between projects, ↑/↓ through a project's versions, Enter or click the centre card to open, Esc closes. "…" forks, renames or deletes (press and hold). Ctrl/⌘+S saves a version.

Scene mode: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan, R auto-rotate.

Object mode (Tab): hover highlights, click selects, drag moves along the floor, Shift+scroll rotates, Alt/⌥+scroll resizes (a redesign), Del hides, H shows all, Esc deselects, K pins the selection in place (any mode; pinned things refuse moving, turning and resizing; a part of a pinned item can still be moved on its own), Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes.

Hands (📷): point (index out, other three fingers curled) and pinch your other hand to select. Make a fist to move the selection along the floor (left/right, push/pull; twist turns it); with nothing selected a fist turns the view. Pinch both hands and pull apart to resize. Lower both hands for 1 s to reset tracking, C recalibrates.

Completed scans (Track B output, with its .json sidecar dropped alongside): filled-in surfaces are drawn ghosted with a hatch, never as measured. I or View switches between completed and as scanned.

Capturing: Scaniverse Object mode for single items, Area mode for rooms; export GLB or OBJ.

Files are read in your browser and never uploaded.
