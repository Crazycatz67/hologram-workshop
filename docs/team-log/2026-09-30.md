### Probe decision rule (finger-gun thumb-drop vs pinch click)
Thumb drop wins only if all hold: median palm shift at most 0.015 frame units (about 11 px); p90 at most 0.03; at most 1.5 times the pinch shift; 4 of 5 drops seen in each orientation; gun detected on at least 80% of still frames.
