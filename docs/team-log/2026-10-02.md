**Goal:** the owner's live test on 2026-10-01 said the hands do not yet feel seamless. Hands v2 rebuilds the input layer (features, one-owner-at-a-time arbiter, new pointer, manipulator and calibration) so gestures feel natural. Plan: `plans/hands-v2/ROADMAP.md`; contract: `plans/hands-v2/CONTRACT.md`. Everything below is behind `?hands=v2`; v1 is unchanged.

**Owner's live feedback (summary):** the gestures "felt like a test" rather than a natural way to work. The tool wheel is the part the owner is proudest of.

**Owner decisions today:**
- **Click** = thumb hammer drop on the gun pose, with a pre-onset rewind (the click lands where you aimed just before the thumb moved).
- **Done** = one open palm held still ~0.6 s, plus an on-screen Done button.
- **Vertical spread** = height-only stretch (not uniform scale).
- **Explode:** a slow close keeps your edits; a clap = back to the original.
- **Tilt:** 1:1 palm frame x1.5, plus ratchet, plus flick, with a soft ~80 degree pitch limit.
- **Aim** = index knuckle direction plus ~20% finger nudge.
- **Cursor:** speed gain plus re-centring replaces the strict absolute cursor.
- **Owner clip recording is postponed to the END** of Hands v2.
